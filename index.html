Legal, Privacy & About Us

About Trip Booker

Trip Booker is Kenya's premier end-to-end travel platform, founded by James Nderi. We connect travelers with seamless booking solutions for flights, hotels, SGR trains, car rentals, cruises, buses, and custom AI-generated itineraries with dedicated 24-hour support.

Privacy Policy & Secure Data

We prioritize the security of your personal and financial information. All M-Pesa transactions and credit card data are encrypted using secure industry-standard protocols. Your data is never sold to third parties and is used solely to manage your travel bookings and account preferences.

Terms & Conditions / User Agreement

By using Trip Booker, you agree to adhere to our cancellation policies, payment terms, and vendor guidelines. Bookings are subject to availability and real-time confirmation through our integrated partner networks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does M-Pesa payment work?
When you checkout with M-Pesa, an STK push is transmitted directly to your Safaricom mobile phone number for instant confirmation.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Yes, all upcoming bookings include instant cancellation options directly from your bookings dashboard.
